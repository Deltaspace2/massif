## Done: `tmb-refuges`

A new source reading the TMB booking portal. Structured (CLAUDE.md rule 6):
every day is a `cal-cell` whose class carries its state, so no model is
involved and nothing depends on rendered text.

**It is the only source in reach that carries French, Italian and Swiss huts in
one structure.** CLAUDE.md records the Italian side as blocked because
`comune.courmayeur.ao.it` does not resolve and the tourist office is the wrong
kind of publisher *for a notice*. This is not a notice source. Availability is
a different claim from a decree, and it is emitted as `open`, never as a
closure.

**What it will not say.** The portal's own legend reads "Complet ou fermé" for
a taken day — full **or** shut, and it refuses to distinguish. Reading that as
closed would invent a closure out of a busy weekend. Only "Disponible" and
"Dernières places" are believed; everything else says nothing, and a hut with
nothing bookable inside four weeks stays unknown.

**Automation.** Index-driven: `/fr/refuges` lists every hut the portal carries,
so one appearing there is picked up with no config change. That is the
difference between this and `hut-sites`, which needs a curated URL per hut.
`robots.txt` is `User-agent: *` with no Disallow, checked today. Trust 0.6,
below every operator and mairie — a booking engine reports what it can sell,
which is a weaker claim about a building than its operator publishing a season.

### The bug the fixture caught, which is the useful part

It first reused `hut_reservation.run_around` — both sources are booking
calendars, so one collapse for both looked obviously right. It is wrong here:
**this portal marks today itself `--past`** and sells from tomorrow, so the run
containing today is empty for every hut on every day. The source would have
emitted nothing, for ever, with a green suite and a dry run that printed rows.

Replaced with `season_from`, which groups bookable days across gaps shorter
than `SEASON_GAP_DAYS` (28) and anchors the window at the observation date
rather than the first bookable day. The second half of that matters as much as
the first: anchoring at the first bookable day publishes a status that is not
in force yet and leaves the hut unknown on the day someone is reading it.

The gap constant does two jobs, both about the same fact — an alpine season
ends for months, not a fortnight. Bertone is `--full` on 641 of its 731 days
and is plainly open in September, so a strict contiguous run gave a one-day
window that expired the same afternoon.

### The 38 refusals are the source working, not failing

Two are near-misses the altitude screen caught, and both would have been wrong
answers of exactly the kind rule 8 exists for:

- `Chalet 'Le Dolent'` at 1634 m matched our `bivouac-du-dolent` at **90** —
  and our Dolent is at 2667 m. A different building, a kilometre below.
- `Refuge de la Balme` at 1706 m matched `refuge-du-col-de-balme` at **90** —
  ours is at 2191 m. Also a different building.

Most of the rest are the ~30 valley gîtes and hotels the portal carries and we
do not track, refused for not reaching the name floor. Correct, but it means a
permanently noisy review queue — see below.

### Three huts blocked on OUR data, not theirs

`refuge-du-fioux` (matched 100), `refuge-le-peuty` (100) and `rifugio-bertone`
(95) were all refused with "our record carries no altitude to check it
against". The two-screen rule refuses rather than letting a name stand alone,
which is right — it is the rule that just caught the two cases above.

Nine active huts have no altitude in `features`, and **none of them has one in
`feature_facts` either**, so there is nothing in the database to fill them from:

    auberge-de-bionnassay, cabane-de-la-tour-rouge, cabane-du-lac-des-vesses,
    la-flegere, refuge-de-tre-la-tete, refuge-du-fioux, refuge-du-montenvers,
    refuge-le-peuty, rifugio-bertone

Taking the altitude from the TMB portal and then validating the TMB match
against it is circular, and taking it from memory is publishing an unchecked
claim.

**OSM was then asked directly, and has no `ele` for any of the nine.** All nine
carry an OSM id and were imported by `import_osm_huts`, which already reads
`ele` into `alt_max` — so the gap is upstream, in OSM, not in our importer. One
Overpass call, all nine objects returned, `ele=None` on every one:

    auberge-de-bionnassay  cabane-de-la-tour-rouge  cabane-du-lac-des-vesses
    la-flegere             refuge-de-tre-la-tete    refuge-du-fioux
    refuge-du-montenvers   refuge-le-peuty          rifugio-bertone

So this is not "not done", it is "no independent source in reach tonight". The
next step is a real one and needs recon: IGN's elevation API can return a
ground elevation for a coordinate, but that is the terrain under the building,
not the altitude its operator publishes, and whether the two are close enough
to screen a name match against is a question that has to be measured before it
is trusted. Worth roughly three more huts immediately, and it improves every
altitude-screened source including ffcam-refuges.
